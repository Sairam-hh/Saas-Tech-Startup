FAQ

Questions, answered

Can I switch plans at any time?

Yes — upgrade or downgrade instantly from your dashboard. Changes prorate automatically, so you never pay for what you don't use.

Is there a free trial?

Every paid plan includes a 14-day free trial with no credit card required. You can explore all features before committing.

Do you offer discounts for startups?

Eligible early-stage startups get 50% off the Growth plan for the first year. Reach out via the contact page to apply.

How secure is my data?

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest with AES-256. We're SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, and HIPAA compliant, with quarterly third-party penetration testing.

Can I export my data?

Absolutely. Your data is yours — export everything to JSON or CSV at any time, with no lock-in.
